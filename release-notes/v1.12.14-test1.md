**Test build.**

- **Auto-dim pauses during HDR.** When the monitor is showing HDR content (HDR video, HDR photos…), the software dimming is switched off and its overlay removed, so the HDR picture keeps its full brightness. Dimming comes back about 3 seconds after the HDR content is gone. Detection uses SurfaceFlinger's HDR layer info for the monitor, plus a forced HDR output mode or an HDR/SDR ratio when the system reports them. HDR switched on only from the monitor's own menu can't be seen by the phone.
- The diagnostic report's `auto-dim:` section now has an `hdr:` line (listener state, HDR layers, what triggered the pause).
- Includes the 1.12.13 "monitor maximum brightness (nits)" field.
