**New since 1.12.10** (same signing key: installs over 1.12.8–1.12.10, no uninstall needed; reboot after updating).

- New option **"Dim the monitor with the light sensor"** (Monitor section, off by default). Android can't change an external monitor's backlight (no DDC/CI access on stock kernels, and most monitors don't expose brightness over USB), so the module darkens the picture in software: a transparent black layer on the monitor that follows the phone's ambient light sensor with a smooth fade. Full picture from about 200 lux, only a little dimming under normal indoor light, most of it in a really dark room.
- Slider **"Maximum dimming in the dark"** (10–90%, default 50%).
- Tip: set the monitor's own brightness to a medium-low level and let the module handle the rest. The diagnostic report shows the current lux and dimming (`auto-dim:` line).
