**Test build.** New: a **main switch** at the top of the module's settings ("Module enabled").

- Off: the monitor goes back to mirroring, the phone screen and the dock go back to normal, and every hook lets HyperOS behave as stock, without a reboot and without touching LSPosed.
- On again: reconnect the monitor and choose Computer (or use the Monitor tile).
- Desktop mode is fully stock again only after reconnecting the monitor or rebooting, because HyperOS reads some of its flags once.
- The Monitor tile shows "Module off" while the switch is off; the diagnostics status line says so too.

Idea from the fork by @oldbkroll. Same signing key as 1.12.8: installs over it.
