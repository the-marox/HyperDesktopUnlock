**Test build.**

- New field **"Monitor maximum brightness (nits)"** (Monitor section, under the auto-dim options). Monitors don't report their brightness, so you can type it from the spec sheet (SDR value). With it set, auto-dim aims at a real brightness instead of a fixed percentage: about 50 nits in a dark room, about 130 under normal indoor light, up to 400 in daylight. A 600-nit monitor is therefore dimmed more than a 250-nit one. Keep the monitor's own brightness at 100%. *Maximum dimming in the dark* still caps the dimming; leave the field empty to keep the 1.12.12 behaviour.
- The diagnostic report's `auto-dim:` line also shows the target brightness.

Same signing key as 1.12.8–1.12.12: installs over them.
