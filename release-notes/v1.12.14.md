**New since 1.12.12** (same signing key: installs over 1.12.8–1.12.12, no uninstall needed; reboot after updating).

- **Auto-dim pauses during HDR.** When the monitor shows HDR content (HDR video, HDR photos…), the software dimming is switched off and its overlay removed, so HDR keeps its full brightness. Dimming comes back about 3 seconds after the HDR content ends. HDR switched on only from the monitor's own menu can't be seen by the phone.
- New field **"Monitor maximum brightness (nits)"** (Monitor section, under the auto-dim options). Type the monitor's SDR brightness from its spec sheet and auto-dim aims at a real brightness instead of a fixed percentage: about 50 nits in a dark room, about 130 under normal indoor light, up to 400 in daylight. Keep the monitor's own brightness at 100%. *Maximum dimming in the dark* still caps the dimming; leave the field empty to keep the 1.12.12 behaviour.
- The diagnostic report's `auto-dim:` section shows the target brightness and an `hdr:` line.
