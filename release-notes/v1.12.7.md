## Fixed
- **Xiaomi 14 (`houji`) and other HyperOS 4 stable builds: the monitor stayed in mirroring after choosing Computer** (#2). On these builds SystemUI accepts the choice but never switches the monitor out of mirroring; the module now does it itself, the same switch the *Monitor* Quick Settings tile makes. Thanks to @CarnivalO for the reports and testing.
- Desktop-mode checks that some builds moved into an interface (`DesktopState`) no longer fail to hook with "Cannot hook abstract methods"; their implementations are looked up instead.

## New
- **Touchpad: back gesture.** With the phone as a touchpad, swipe in from the left or right edge to go Back on the monitor. On by default, can be turned off in the settings.
- The diagnostic report now logs SystemUI's writes of `mirror_built_in_display`, to tell apart "mirroring forced back" from "never switched".
