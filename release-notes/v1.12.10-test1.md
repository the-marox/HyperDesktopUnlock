**Test build.** Includes the main switch from 1.12.9-test1, plus:

- New option **"Don't open HyperOS's own desktop on the monitor"** (Dock section, off by default). It keeps the stock Xiaomi desktop (`SecondaryDisplayLauncher`) off the monitor and closes it if it is already open. Try it if the phone's gestures stop working after you change the monitor's density or scale. The dock and your apps are not affected; without a dock the monitor shows only the wallpaper until you open an app.
- **Simplified Chinese** translation of the module's settings.

Ideas and first tests from the fork by @oldbkroll. Same signing key as 1.12.8/1.12.9: installs over them.
